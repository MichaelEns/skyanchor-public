SkyAnchor

A personal web/PWA fork maintained by Michael Ens, not a Bluesky Social PBC product. Open SkyAnchor.

Privacy and retention

The upstream account session is stored in your browser. Following reading data is account-scoped: a local window of at most 90 occurrences and 1 MiB, a within-post offset, up to eight neighbors, and a separate bounded Return checkpoint. It is not an encrypted vault or complete archive.

Private sync is optional. Connect private sync once per device to consent to debounced cloud saves. The same-origin Worker verifies a bsky.social access token without receiving your password or refresh token. D1 stores your verified DID, device UUID, reading occurrence/offset/neighbors, revision and update time. Session rows contain a token hash, CSRF token and ten-minute expiry, not a stored access token.

Expired sessions are removed on the next session creation and may remain physically stored until then. Cloud progress has no automatic expiry. Delete cloud progress and disconnect removes progress while retaining a revision tombstone to reject stale writes; your local place is unchanged. Disconnect other devices too to stop their future saves. Signing out clears this device's account reading/Return data and revokes its own sync session. Tombstones and the monotonic clock have no TTL.

No remote analytics delivery is enabled. The service worker does not cache responses, private APIs, media or feeds. Internet connectivity is required for cold startup; background delivery on iPhone is not guaranteed. Bluesky, your PDS, appview, chat, video, media, moderation and age-assurance providers receive their normal service requests, and Cloudflare has its own infrastructure policies. Terms and independent providers

SkyAnchor is a personal fork distributed as-is without an uptime or support guarantee. Its code uses the upstream MIT license; artwork and fonts have separate licenses. Code license and third-party notices accompany this export. SkyAnchor is not endorsed by Bluesky Social PBC and cannot resolve provider account appeals.

Your accounts and posts remain subject to your providers' rules, including the Bluesky terms and community guidelines. Normal moderation and age-assurance requirements are not bypassed.

Support and installation

Report SkyAnchor bugs through SkyAnchor public issues. This source repository may not yet be published. Do not include passwords or tokens in a report. For provider account issues, use the Bluesky provider help desk.

Windows: open SkyAnchor in Edge or Chrome; optionally install it from the browser's app-install menu. iPhone: open it in Safari, then Share, Add to Home Screen, and Open as Web App when offered. Sign in and enable private sync separately in each browser/app storage context. There is no native store app, web push or offline whole-feed cache.

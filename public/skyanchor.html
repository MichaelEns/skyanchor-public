SkyAnchor

A personal web/PWA fork maintained by Michael Ens, not a Bluesky Social PBC product. Open SkyAnchor.

Privacy and retention

The upstream account session is stored in your browser. Following reading data is account-scoped: a local window of at most 90 occurrences and 1 MiB, a within-post offset, up to eight neighbors, and a separate bounded Return checkpoint. It is not an encrypted vault or complete archive.

Private sync is optional. Connect private sync once per device to consent to debounced cloud saves. The same-origin Worker verifies a bsky.social access token without receiving your password or refresh token. D1 stores your verified DID, device UUID, reading occurrence/offset/neighbors, revision, server save time and private browser-profile names. Names may contain personal information: choose nonsensitive, replaceable labels. Websites cannot read OS machine names. Default names are approximate browser/platform families with a random profile-ID suffix; a browser profile is not a physical machine. Session rows contain a token hash, CSRF token and ten-minute expiry, not a stored access token.

Expired sessions are removed on the next session creation and may remain physically stored until then. Cloud progress has no time-based expiry. Each browser profile/feed retains its latest confirmed save and two older distinct occurrences, not unlimited history; saving the same occurrence again replaces its offset and time. Up to 32 browser profiles may register per account, without silent eviction. Names have no automatic expiry and can be replaced by renaming that browser. Save times are not post ages or evidence of last device use: a recent save can point to an old post. Delete cloud progress and disconnect removes the active feed's progress and all its browser-profile save history while retaining names, other feeds and a revision tombstone to reject stale writes; your local place is unchanged. Disconnect other devices too to stop their future saves. Signing out clears this device's account reading/Return data and revokes its own sync session. Tombstones and the monotonic clock have no TTL.

Latest shared remains the default Check/Resume source. Selecting or checking a browser's saved place does not move the feed. Explicit Resume saved place restores locally without changing latest shared; sharing stays paused until explicit Save my place or Keep mine. Deleted, moderated or expired occurrences may be unavailable. Recovery is not a guaranteed timeline archive. Signing out clears cached device names/history from the UI, but does not delete cloud history or names.

No remote analytics delivery is enabled. The service worker does not cache responses, private APIs, media or feeds. Internet connectivity is required for cold startup; background delivery on iPhone is not guaranteed. Bluesky, your PDS, appview, chat, video, media, moderation and age-assurance providers receive their normal service requests, and Cloudflare has its own infrastructure policies. Support and installation

Report SkyAnchor bugs through SkyAnchor public issues. This source repository may not yet be published. Do not include passwords or tokens in a report. For provider account issues, use the Bluesky provider help desk.

Windows: open SkyAnchor in Edge or Chrome; optionally install it from the browser's app-install menu. iPhone: open it in Safari, then Share, Add to Home Screen, and Open as Web App when offered. Sign in and enable private sync separately in each browser/app storage context. There is no native store app, web push or offline whole-feed cache.
